## 3. Dispatch More: what was built in v248 / v249, in detail

### 3.1 Server rewind (`fork/pause-resume.md` §4.3)

**Why.** The user: pausing live TV for 20-30 minutes and coming back played *live*. Root cause:
arrTV's Live Rewind keeps its recording on the TV, and its budget is
`TimeshiftBufferStore.freeSpaceBudgetBytes()` = free space minus 2 GB. The Shield had **1.0 GB
free**, so the ring was one segment deep and every resume landed at live (the code's own log
line says so: "A full disk leaves the buffer one segment deep: a pause then resumes at live").
The user: "devices don't have enough space, what can we build on the server side?" -> the server
records the channel instead.

**Files.**
- `apps/proxy/live_proxy/rewind.py` (new). Constants: `USER_AGENT="DispatchMore-Rewind/1"`,
  `SEGMENT_SECONDS=6`, `VIEWER_TTL=45`, `LEASE_TTL=30`, `LOOP=5`, `FILES_GRACE=300`,
  `DISK_MARGIN_BYTES=5 GB`, `MAX_RESTARTS=20`.
  - `settings()` from `app_devices.load_settings()`: `rewind`, `rewind_minutes`,
    `rewind_max_pause_minutes`, `rewind_budget_gb`.
  - `rewind_dir()` -- env `DISPATCHARR_REWIND_DIR`, default `/data/rewind`; `stream_base()` --
    env `DISPATCHARR_INTERNAL_URL`, default `http://127.0.0.1:9191`.
  - `watch(uuid, viewer, paused_at_ms)`: records the viewer in Redis
    (`rewind:viewers:<uuid>`, 45 s), and `ensure_recorder` starts the recorder unless the lease
    `rewind:owner:<uuid>` is held by another worker (one recorder per channel across uWSGI
    workers). `leave`, `viewers`, `keep_from_ms` (the last `rewind_minutes`, or a paused TV's
    position minus 30 s, within `rewind_max_pause_minutes`).
  - `Recorder`: ffmpeg
    `-loglevel fatal -user_agent DispatchMore-Rewind/1 -i <base>/proxy/ts/stream/<uuid>
    -map 0:v? -map 0:a? -c copy -f hls -hls_time 6 -hls_list_size 0
    -hls_flags program_date_time+temp_file+independent_segments
    -hls_segment_filename p<part>-%06d.ts part-<part>.m3u8`; stderr to `recorder.log` (a pipe
    nobody reads fills and stalls ffmpeg); dies with its worker (`PR_SET_PDEATHSIG`); each
    restart is a new *part*. `_supervise` every 5 s: restarts ffmpeg while viewers exist (max 20),
    kills it at once when none (it holds the provider connection), deletes the folder 5 min
    later, trims, enforces the budget, refreshes the lease.
  - `segments()` parses the parts' own playlists (sorted by part, wall time), `playlist()` makes
    an **EVENT** playlist with `#EXT-X-PROGRAM-DATE-TIME` per segment and
    `#EXT-X-DISCONTINUITY` between parts, MEDIA-SEQUENCE from the wall time; `window()`,
    `segment_path()` (names must match `^p(\d+)-(\d+)\.ts$`), `trim`, `budget_bytes`
    (never more than free space minus 5 GB), `enforce_budget` (oldest segments of channels
    nobody is paused on go first), `usage()`.
- `apps/proxy/live_proxy/rewind_views.py`: `rewind_channel` (GET window / POST watch
  `{viewer, paused_at}` / DELETE leave; IsStandardUser; viewer key `f"{user.id}:{viewer}"`),
  `rewind_usage` (IsAdmin), `rewind_playlist` and `rewind_segment` (AllowAny +
  `network_access_allowed`, like streams).
- URLs: `live_proxy/urls.py` `rewind/<uuid>/index.m3u8`, `rewind/<uuid>/<name>` (so
  `/proxy/ts/rewind/...`); `apps/channels/api_urls.py` `rewind/`, `rewind/<uuid>/`
  (`/api/channels/rewind/...`).
- `apps/channels/captions/__init__.py`: `HELPER_USER_AGENTS = (CAPTIONS_USER_AGENT,
  "DispatchMore-Rewind/")`; `is_caption_client` covers both, so Force Close and the viewer checks
  pass the recorder by.
- `app_devices.py`: DEFAULTS `rewind: True`, `rewind_minutes: 60` (5-240),
  `rewind_max_pause_minutes: 240` (15-1440), `rewind_budget_gb: 20` (1-4000) (`BOUNDS` dict for
  ints), `look_back_priority: True`, `look_back_priority_notify: True`; `capabilities()` adds
  `rewind`, `rewind_url "/api/channels/rewind/"`, `captions_url`, `look_back_priority`.
- Frontend `ArrTvSettings.jsx`: Server rewind switch, minutes / longest pause / budget,
  `RewindUsage` (what is recorded, its size); `api.js getRewindUsage`.

**Bugs found while building it.** Segments named by the second (`%s`) overwrote each other in
ffmpeg's start burst -> numbered `%06d`. stderr on a PIPE stalled ffmpeg -> file. In tests the
module-level `_recorders` leaked between tests -> cleared in setUp.

**Checked.** A real ffmpeg against a served sample: segments, parts, playlist, stop and clean-up
when the viewer goes. Tests `apps/proxy/live_proxy/tests/test_rewind.py` (10). **Not yet checked
on the real server with the Shield** (§8).

**Disk on the user's server:** ~9.5 GB free -> about 4.5 GB usable after the 5 GB margin.

  - `COOLDOWN_SECONDS=300`, `MOVED_RECENTLY_SECONDS=600`, `VERIFY_SECONDS=10`, `ROOM_TTL=120`.
  - Redis keys: `timeshift:api-room:<session>` (the steps), `timeshift:priority-cooldown:<user>:<channel>`,
    `timeshift:priority-moved:<channel uuid>`.
  - `settings()` (`look_back_priority`, `look_back_priority_notify` from app_devices).
  - `_catchup_profiles(channel)`: the active profiles of every account keeping the archive.
  - `has_room` (`pool_has_capacity_for_profile` on any of them).
  - `candidates(channel, redis, user_id)`: live channels (`probation._active_channels`) on those
    profiles, not recorded, not moved in the last 10 min, **with viewers other than the asker**
    (uncommitted change), that have a non-custom stream on *another* account whose profile has
    room; fewest viewers first.
  - `make_room(user, channel, redis)` is called by the mint **before** it makes the session:
    off or room -> `None` (stock; the cooldown is deleted); **nobody else holds the archive's
    providers -> `None` (stock)** (uncommitted, `others_holding`); cooldown running -> 409;
    no candidate -> 409 + cooldown; else `("moving", found)`.
  - `run_move` (in a gevent greenlet via `start_move`): `found` -> `moving` -> `_switch`
    (`get_stream_info_for_switch` + `ChannelService.change_stream_url`, the same switch Channel
    Switch Overlap's `_migrate_to_free_profile` uses) -> `moved` -> `verify` (metadata names the
    new stream, state `active`, `buffer_index` grows, within 10 s) -> `verified` -> `ready` if
    the archive profile has room. Not playing: `reverting`, switch back, verify, `refused` +
    cooldown. A socket message `{"type": "lookback_moved", "channel", "stream", "text"}` on
    `updates` tells the moved TV (when `look_back_priority_notify`).
- `apps/timeshift/api_views.py`: the mint returns **409** `{error, reason: "viewing_priorities",
  retry_after}` or **202** `{…session…, state: "making_room", room, room_url}`; new

- Tests `apps/timeshift/tests/test_priority.py` (7 committed, + 2 in the uncommitted change:

### 3.3 Caption translation (`fork/subtitles.md` §9 step 4)

**Why.** The user: "subtitles work but there is no translation yet".

- `apps/channels/captions/translate.py` (new): `translated(answer, channel_uuid, lang, settings,
  redis_client)` on the poll's answer. Per (channel, language) a Redis hash
  `captions:tr:<uuid>:<lang>` (seq -> text, 10 min); one TV translates a piece at a time
  (`:lock`, 20 s); lines go out **in order** (an untranslated line holds back the later ones until
  the next poll); each cue keeps `original`; `translation: {to, from, state, engine}`; states
  `translated`, `same language`, `waiting for the language` (no cues until the model settles on
  the programme's language), `not available`, `failed` (both with the original lines + `reason`).
  Engines (`engine()`): the card's `translator` ("" automatic, `deepl`, `ollama`, `opus-mt`,
  `off`): DeepL (`api-free.deepl.com` for keys ending `:fx`), Ollama `/api/generate` asked for a
  JSON list one line per line (`ollama_model`, or its first), the worker's `POST /translate`.
- `worker.py`: Opus-MT -- `opus_pair` reads the pair's Hugging Face card
  (`https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-<pair>/raw/main/README.md`), downloads the
  original Marian zip from `object.pouta.csc.fi`, converts with CTranslate2's `OpusMTConverter`
  (int8, no PyTorch needed), keeps `source.spm` / `target.spm`; `translate_lines` direct pair,
  else through English; `can_translate()` in `/status`. **Checked here:** nl-en is 82 MB and
  translated two lines in 0.04 s on a CPU ("Goedenavond en welkom bij het journaal." -> "Good
  evening and welcome to the news."); nl-de through English worked (first use ~2 min for two
  downloads).
- `live.py`: `poll(channel, since, lang)`; `subtitles_views.captions_live` passes
  `request.GET["lang"]`.
- `manager.py`: DEFAULTS `translator: ""`, `ollama_model: ""`; `sentencepiece` added to the
  Docker worker packages; `fork/patcher/captions.sh` installs `sentencepiece` after
  faster-whisper.
- CaptionsCard: "Translate captions" select and "Ollama model".
- Tests `apps/channels/tests/test_caption_translation.py` (9).
- **The user must reinstall the worker from the Subtitles tab** for `sentencepiece` (Opus-MT);

## 6. Things learnt that are easy to get wrong

- A **416** on a look back is the provider's archive length shifting between requests; hiding the
  length (UnboundedLength) made it behave like live -- reverted; retry instead.
- Look-back archives have **PTS seams** (e.g. 2469 -> 95443 s, and steps back of 45 s and 10 s)
  and **unaligned TS packets**: the cause of freezes at 2:01 and grey-with-audio before arr.83.
- A **503** on a look back is usually the provider's only connection being in use (Digitalizard
  allows one) -- not the release speed of the previous connection (an early guess that was
  wrong and corrected).
- Digitalizard has archives (Food Network UK 2 days); TiviBridge has archives for some channels.
- The Stats page's dormant "Could Not Dispatch" channel: the fallback slate, kept alive by a
  client (another arrTV) for 12+ hours.
- The server's fingerprint matching (stock) reuses the viewer's pool session when they hop to
  another programme of the **same channel** (one provider connection per viewer) -- log lines
  "Timeshift fingerprint matched session X for Y" / "takeover: displacing". That is by design.
- Several sessions overlapping for a few seconds while jumping is normal (mint new, delete old).
