**For Dispatcharr 0.33.0.** Stream Check no longer uses up what your providers allow:

- **Providers limit how many streams may be opened in a while** (one allows 6 every 15
  minutes). Stream Check counted only its own openings and used most of that allowance, all day;
  once it was gone the provider refused every channel anyone tried. Now your own channel starts
  count too, the check keeps to half of what a provider allows, and it leaves a provider alone
  for 20 minutes after anyone starts a channel there.
- **A channel start on a provider that is at its limit moves on at once** to the next provider,
  instead of asking three times -- every try counts against that limit.
- A full round can take days on a strict provider: giving Stream Check a night window in its
  settings is a good idea if you watch much. The window is in the server's own time.

Install with the one command, as usual.
